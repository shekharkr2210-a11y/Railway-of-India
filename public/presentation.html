3. AI Core 1: Task Criticality Index (TCI) Algorithm

Multi-criteria weighted heuristic ranking engine for track defects

TCI Mathematical Formula

TCI = w₁·Severity + w₂·Overdue + w₃·SpeedImpact + w₄·PowerFactor

  • • Defect Severity (w₁): CRITICAL (45 pts), HIGH (35 pts), MEDIUM (25 pts)
  • • Overdue Days (w₂): +3.5 pts per day overdue (max 30 pts)
  • • Speed Restriction Impact (w₃): Restoring 30 km/h to 130 km/h adds high score
  • • Power Block (w₄): +5 pts if 25kV OHE isolation is required

Operational Benefits

  • ✔ Prevents Fractures: High-TCI rail flaws (Score > 90) get instant slot priority before structural failure.
  • ✔ Restores Line Speed: Eliminates temporary speed restrictions (TSRs) to maintain express train throughput.
  • ✔ Eliminates Overdue Backlog: Escalates task scores automatically every 24 hours.
Student Pitch Speech: "Coordinator, TCI is a multi-criteria heuristic scoring function. With thousands of defect reports logged across 18 zones every day, human controllers cannot manually rank which defect is most dangerous. TCI ranks every single task objectively in milliseconds!"

5. Optimization Math & Safety Constraints

Mixed-integer optimization formulation adhering strictly to Indian Railways G&SR safety rules

Objective Function

min ( α·TrainDelays + β·UnmetPriority + γ·Downtime - δ·ShadowSavings )

  • • Minimizes passenger & freight train delay penalties
  • • Penalizes postponing high-TCI urgent defects
  • • Minimizes total track downtime duration
  • • Rewards co-locating multi-department maintenance tasks

Hard Physical Constraints

  • 1. Spatial Proximity: Merged tasks must be within |Km_A - Km_B| ≤ 8 KM.
  • 2. Safety Buffer: Mandatory 500m separation between heavy machines & human crews.
  • 3. Power Interlock: OHE de-energization must cover the entire spatial span of TRD work.
  • 4. Timetable Protection: Zero block allocation during high-priority Vande Bharat / Rajdhani passings.
