1. The Problem: Uncoordinated Departmental Silos

How railway maintenance is traditionally planned without AI integration

3 Separate Department Requests

  • •
    Civil Track (TMS): Requests 2.5 hours track blockage for rail relaying.
  • •
    Electrical TRD (TDMS): Requests 2.0 hours power block for OHE wire check.
  • •
    Signal S&T (SMMS): Requests 1.5 hours disconnection for point machines.

Operational Consequences

  • ❌
    Fragmented Track Closure: 3 separate line blockages on the same corridor section per day.
  • ❌
    6.0 Hours Total Downtime: Track rendered unusable for passenger & freight trains.
  • ❌
    Frequent Delays: Major passenger train slow-downs and goods train stagnation.
Student Key Lesson: In enterprise engineering, system failures often occur not because individual teams are incompetent, but because data is siloed without unified optimization algorithms.

3. AI Task Criticality Index (TCI) Algorithm

Weighted scoring function for prioritizing thousands of maintenance alerts

TCI = w₁ · Severity + w₂ · OverdueDays + w₃ · SpeedImpact + w₄ · PowerFactor

Weight Factors Breakdown

  • • Severity (w₁): CRITICAL (45 pts), HIGH (35 pts), MEDIUM (25 pts).
  • • Overdue Days (w₂): 3.5 pts per day overdue (max 30 pts).
  • • Speed Restriction (w₃): 0.4 pts per km/h speed reduction avoided.
  • • Power Factor (w₄): +5 pts if 25kV OHE power block required.

Practical Benefits

  • • Safety Priority: High-risk rail fractures (TCI > 90) get immediate block scheduling.
  • • Line Speed Restoration: Eliminates temporary speed restrictions (TSR) slowing trains.
  • • Algorithmic Objectivity: Prevents department bias in scheduling.
Student Key Lesson: This is a classic multi-criteria decision making (MCDM) heuristic model used in Artificial Intelligence to evaluate risk dynamically.

5. Optimization Math & Physical Constraints

Formulating track block scheduling as a Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP)

Objective Cost Function

min ( α·TrainDelays + β·UnmetPriority + γ·Downtime - δ·ShadowSavings )

  • • TrainDelays: Total passenger/freight delay hours created.
  • • UnmetPriority: Penalty for delaying high-TCI critical tasks.
  • • Downtime: Total track closure duration.
  • • ShadowSavings: Reward multiplier for co-locating tasks.

Hard Constraints

  • 1. Spatial Proximity: Task overlap distance |Km_A - Km_B| ≤ 8 KM.
  • 2. Safety Headway: Minimum 500m separation between heavy machinery and human workers.
  • 3. Power Isolation: 25kV OHE power block must cover full geographical bounds of TRD work.
  • 4. Timetable Protection: Zero interference with High-Priority Express trains (Vande Bharat / Rajdhani).
Student Key Lesson: This is Operations Research (OR) in practice! Algorithms like Branch-and-Bound and Constraint Satisfaction Solvers resolve these NP-hard scheduling problems.
